AREA OF A CIRCLE

Problem: Read the radius and calculate the area of a circle.

Formula:
area = π × radius × radius
Using π ≈ 3.14:
area = 3.14 × radius × radius

Example:
Input: 2
Output: 12.56

Note: In Java, 3.14 is a double literal. Use double for the area, or write 3.14f when assigning to a float.

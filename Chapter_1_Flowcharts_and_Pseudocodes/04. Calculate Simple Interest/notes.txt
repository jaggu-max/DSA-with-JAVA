CALCULATE SIMPLE INTEREST

Problem: Calculate simple interest using principal P, rate R, and time T.

Formula:
SI = (P × R × T) / 100

Algorithm:
1. Start.
2. Input P, R, and T.
3. Calculate SI = (P * R * T) / 100.
4. Print SI.
5. Stop.

Pseudocode:
START
INPUT P, R, T
SI = (P * R * T) / 100
PRINT SI
STOP

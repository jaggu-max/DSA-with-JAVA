WHAT ARE FLOWCHARTS?

A flowchart is a diagram that represents the steps of an algorithm using standard symbols connected by arrows.

Common uses:
- Understand the order of steps.
- Show decisions and loops clearly.
- Plan a solution before writing code.

Typical symbols:
- Oval: Start/End
- Parallelogram: Input/Output
- Rectangle: Process/calculation
- Diamond: Decision (Yes/No)
- Arrow: Direction of flow
